The Emperor of Mankind is locked in a desperate struggle to stabilize a breach in the Eldar Webway—the key to humanity’s future. As the Custodian Guard and the Sisters of Silence battle endless tides of daemons, the story offers a rare and haunting glimpse into the mind of the Emperor Himself, revealing the cold reality of His vision and the devastating price of His Great Work.

Reviews

Harold Bloom

"It is a work of overflowing imagination that, despite its commercial shackles, manages to capture the agony of a god who discovers, too late, that even the purest reason can be devoured by myth."

Marcel Proust Leovigildo Vargas Segundo

"It is a crystal cathedral built over a nightmare abyss; a book that understands that the true horror is not death, but the certainty that even our vastest dreams are condemned to be misunderstood by those who will follow."

Arturo Pérez-Reverte

"Es una crónica de una resistencia inútil y soberbia; el retrato de un hombre solo contra la marea, que comprende que la historia no la escriben los vencedores, sino los que mueren con las botas puestas sabiendo que todo estaba perdido desde el principio."
